[Release notes and checksums](https://github.com/mik799/Vizora-releases/releases/latest)

## What's new

- Simple photo motion: place an anchor, place and drag a movement point, preview, then apply. Automatic area selection and background cleanup; manual controls remain under Advanced. Photo Animation is **Alpha**.
- Video and Reel Maker tabs. Turn a selected time range into a 9:16 portrait reel, preserve lyrics/audio/overlays, replace reel media, and keep the original video separate. Both workspaces save together.
- Six interface themes under Settings → Appearance.
- Fit to screen or Fill screen for new and existing media.
- Native media dragging into empty timelines and existing layers, with a visible drop indicator.
- Selection boxes follow rendered elements, text and visualizers, including circles and offset dotted lines.
- 2K/QHD, 4K and 8K landscape and portrait resolutions.

Photo Animation needs graphics acceleration. Automatic background cleanup and eyelids are approximate; detailed scenery or large movements may need Advanced adjustments. Reel adaptation preserves relative placement but does not detect faces: review portrait framing before exporting. 8K requires more memory, disk space and processing time.

Updates are verified with Vizora's updater signature before installation. Unsaved work may be lost when installing. The installer does not yet have a Windows Authenticode certificate.
